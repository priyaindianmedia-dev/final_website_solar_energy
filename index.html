What is Poweris?

A platform for ecological energy production and management. Communities run their own decentralised power exchange, monitor it themselves and adapt it to local needs.

✧

Energy decentralization

Every rooftop and turbine becomes a node. No single point of failure, no distant utility.

💡

Eco-friendly sustainability

Balances fossil and renewable sources for the smallest possible impact on the environment.

⚡

Power exchange

Sell surplus to neighbours and buy clean energy at fair, transparent prices.
